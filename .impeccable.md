## Design Context

### Users
High-net-worth individuals, expatriates, and real estate investors looking for premium properties in Côte d'Ivoire. They value exclusivity, security, and architectural beauty. They are often browsing on high-end mobile devices or large desktop screens.

### Brand Personality
Cinematic, Immersive, Museum-grade.
The interface should feel like a high-end architectural magazine where the property is the start.

### Aesthetic Direction
Luxury Editorial.
- **Visual Tone**: Refined minimalism with bold typographic statements.
- **Theme**: Light-dominant with deep navy (Primary) and burnt orange (Secondary) accents.
- **References**: Architectural Digest, high-end fashion editorials.
- **Anti-references**: Low-cost listing sites, crowded dashboards, generic real estate templates.

### Design Principles
1. **Focus on the Frame**: Use generous white space to let imagery breathe.
2. **Typographic Hierarchy**: High contrast between display and body text using non-standard scales.
3. **Tactile Interaction**: Transitions should feel smooth and "physical" (springs, parallax).
4. **Cinematic Motion**: Every scroll interaction should reveal a piece of the narrative.
