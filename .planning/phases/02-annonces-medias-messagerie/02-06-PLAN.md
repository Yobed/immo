**IMPORTANT DESIGN DECISION:** BienForm is used for CREATION (no bienId yet). Step5Medias requires `bienId`. Therefore Step5Medias cannot be embedded inside BienForm itself — it must be rendered OUTSIDE of BienForm in the modifier page, triggered by `?step=medias`. The correct approach:

**In `apps/web/components/bien/BienForm/index.tsx`:**

1. Change `TOTAL_STEPS = 4` to `TOTAL_STEPS = 5` so the progress bar shows 5 steps.

2. Update the step 4 → step 5 transition text. On step 4 (Équipements), the "Suivant" button logic currently advances to step 5 which is now "Médias". However since Step5Medias is rendered outside BienForm on the modifier page, step 5 is the submit step — when `step < TOTAL_STEPS` and step is 4, clicking "Suivant" goes to step 5 where the submit button appears. But at step 5 inside BienForm we render nothing for medias — the submit button is at `step === TOTAL_STEPS` (step 5), which means step 4 shows "Suivant" → step 5 shows the "Enregistrer le bien" submit button. This keeps the correct flow: 4 info steps → submit → redirect to ?step=medias page where Step5Medias is shown.

   So the only change in `BienForm/index.tsx` is:
   ```tsx
   const TOTAL_STEPS = 5
   ```
   And add a placeholder render for step 5 inside the form to show a summary/prompt:
   ```tsx
   {step === 5 && (
     <div className="text-center py-6">
       <p className="font-display text-xl text-[var(--text)] mb-2">Prêt à publier ?</p>
       <p className="font-sans text-muted text-sm">Vous pourrez ajouter des photos, vidéos et une vue 360° à l&apos;étape suivante.</p>
     </div>
   )}
   ```
   Add this BEFORE the `<div className="flex justify-between mt-8">` navigation block.

   Change the submit button label from "Enregistrer le bien" to "Continuer vers les médias" since the form now continues to the media step on the modifier page:
   ```tsx
   <Button type="submit" className="ml-auto" loading={isSubmitting}>
     Continuer vers les médias
   </Button>
   ```
